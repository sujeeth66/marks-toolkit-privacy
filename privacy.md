Privacy Policy: Marks Portal Toolkit
Last updated: 19/10/2026

What this extension is
Marks Portal Toolkit is an independent project made by G.Sujeeth Madhav. It is not an official product of the Government of Telangana or the Department of School Education. It is used by teachers to prepare marks sheets and enter marks on the CCE marks entry page of the Telangana school education portal (schooledu.telangana.gov.in). It works only on that page.

Information the extension uses
- Student ID and name, and the class, section, subject and exam shown on the marks entry page.
- Marks and attendance, and photographs of completed mark sheets or completed Excel workbooks, when the teacher chooses to upload them.

How the information is used
When a teacher clicks a button, the extension sends the information it needs to our processing service, which is hosted on Render. The service creates the sheets, reads the photographs, or checks the workbook, and returns the result to the teacher's browser. The extension writes marks into the portal page only. It never saves or submits the portal form. The teacher reviews and saves the page.

What is not kept
Student names, IDs, marks, attendance and photographs are processed in memory or in temporary files. Temporary files are deleted as soon as the response is sent. The service keeps no database of students or marks.

Logs
The service records only the type of an error and the address called, never the contents of a request. The hosting provider keeps its own standard access records, such as time and network address, to run the service.

Usage limits
To prevent misuse, the service limits how many requests a device and a network address can make per minute. The extension stores a random identifier on the teacher's computer for this purpose. It contains no personal information.

Other things we do not do
We do not require accounts. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not sell information or share it with anyone other than the hosting provider.
